Selfless service for neglected, abandoned and poverty-stricken families in Panjab — food every month, homes made safe, and school fees for children who would otherwise stop.

Why Your Seva?

Serving the unfortunate across the globe selflessly.

£15 feeds a family for a month. Most households we visit have no income at all.

You can help open the door to food, shelter, dignity and education — especially for elders living alone and children whose schooling is about to end.

Give today

100%

Thanks to Gift Aid and a small circle of private donors who cover our running costs, 100% of your donation goes directly to projects in Panjab — food, home rebuilding, winter care and school fees.
